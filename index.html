Who We Are

A consultancy built on one idea — no client should feel lost inside Thai law.

Siam Consult Phuket is a legal, immigration and corporate advisory firm working throughout Thailand. We represent private clients, foreign investors and established companies — from a first tourist-visa extension to a full corporate restructure or a contested case in court.

Our practice pairs licensed Thai lawyers with bilingual consultants, so every document filed in Thai is understood in your own language before you sign it. We keep the same team on your file from the first consultation to the final stamp — no handovers, no unexplained fees, no surprises.

How We Work

Four steps. No guesswork.

Every engagement follows the same disciplined path, whether it is a single visa extension or a multi-year corporate mandate.

01

Consultation

A private session — in person, or by video — where we map your situation and tell you honestly what is and is not possible under Thai law.

02

Strategy & Fixed Quote

You receive a written plan, the documents required, the realistic timeline and a fixed fee. Nothing begins until you approve it.
